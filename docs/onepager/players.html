Session Hub How it works

Your calls, in one place.

Session Hub is where you book your call with Winning Minds, and your private line to Michal and Milan in between. It takes about a minute to learn.

Everything is shown in Madison time. Prague is six or seven hours ahead depending on the month, and the app has already done that sum. Book the time you see.

Getting in

Go to winningmindsperformance.com and sign in with the email and password Mára sent you.

Change that password straight away under Account → Change your password. Pick something you do not use anywhere else.

Booking a call

  1. Open My calls.
  2. Tap a green time, like 8:00 AM ○○
  3. Check the details and tap Confirm booking.

That is it — you are booked. First come, first served.

What the dots mean

Michal and Milan both run calls, so one time can hold two places.

○○ both free   ●○ one left   ●● gone

A time with no dots has a single place.

Changing your mind

Your booked call is the red one, marked Yours. Tap it to give it back, and somebody else can take it.

You hold one call per week. Booking another in the same week moves you — the app will say so before it does.

Joining on the day

The Google Meet link appears on the Your next call card at the top of My calls. The Join button opens ten minutes before the start.

If it still says the link is coming, it is on its way — message us if the call is close.

Messages

Your thread under Messages goes to Michal and Milan and nobody else. Not your teammates, not your coaches.

You can send a file with it too.

Tasks and Resources

Tasks are what we agree between calls. Tick one off when it is done.

Resources holds anything we have shared with you to read or watch.

Who sees what

  • Teammates see only each other’s names on the calendar — nothing else.
  • Coaches see who booked and who turned up. They never see what is said in a call, in a message, or in a note.
  • Winning Minds sees your bookings, your messages and your tasks.
